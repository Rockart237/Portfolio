About Me

My Computer Engineering Journey

Hello! I am John Enoch Isidro Espinola, an aspiring Computer Engineering student at Mapúa University. My fascination with computers extends beyond software applications, I am deeply motivated by the human curiosity to take apart things and put them back together in different ways beyond software and hardware.

At Mapúa, Im excited to immerse myself in both hardware systems and programming fundamentals. From designing basic logic gates to configuring microcontrollers and implementing data structures, I thrive in environments that require logical rigour, problem solving, and analytical thinking.

Parallel to my academic studies, my experience as an Online English Tutor at RareJob has sharpened my communication skills, patience, and cross-cultural adaptability qualities that complement my technical skillset in collaborative engineering projects.

Entering Mapúa University & Choosing CpE

Choosing Mapúa University and taking Computer Engineering was influenced by my curiosity as it is regarded as one of, if not, the best engineering school in the Philippines. I was drawn to the program's balance of hardware and software, which aligns with my interest in both areas. The decision was also motivated by the desire to graduate early and have an alma mater I can boast about.
